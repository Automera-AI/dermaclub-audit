The October change

Meta starts charging per message. We cut that bill by ~85%.

From 1 October 2026, Meta charges for each WhatsApp reply a business sends. Left unmanaged, at your volume that is roughly $2,722/month (EGP 137,500) in Meta fees alone. This is the change every clinic on WhatsApp is about to feel.

Here is the part most people miss: when a patient reaches you by tapping a Click-to-WhatsApp button on a Facebook or Instagram ad, Meta opens a 72-hour window where replies are free. You already run those ads. We route them through the native WhatsApp destination so eligible conversations open that free window, and we combine three fragmented replies into one clean message.
